schema Calibrated Triage Priority Queues

RED QUEUE (Critical Functional Defect, s ≥ τ) Manual Review

Trace opens and net bridges confirmed by topological differential graph. Halts conveyor for immediate visual disposition.

YELLOW QUEUE (Boundary Uncertainty, τ - ε ≤ s < τ) Auto Flying Probe

Marginal optical evidence near calibration threshold. Auto-routed to 4-point flying probe electrical testing or active learning telemetry.

GREEN QUEUE (Bypassed Cosmetic Flaw, s < τ - ε) Auto Cleared

Minor pin-holes, mousebites, and non-functional copper spurs. Cleared automatically without stopping the manufacturing conveyor.

Cost Decision Crossover ($R^*=1,203$)

$\text{Cost} = \text{Reviews} + R \cdot \text{Escapes}$ where $R = C_{\text{escape}} / C_{\text{review}}$

Aggregate Pareto Dominance:
Under pooled, shift-inflated, and board-median calibration, Policy B reduces both review workload and critical escapes in aggregate.
Worst-Board Break-Even Derivation:
Workload drops from $12.10 \to 8.09$ (saving $4,010$ reviews/1k boards), while escapes shift $80.2 \to 83.6$ ($+3.34$ escapes):
$3.333 \cdot R < 4,010.22 \implies R^* = 1,203.07$
Policy B is cost-preferred for $R < 1,203$. For standard manufacturing ($R \in [50, 500]$), PCBRisk provides substantial net savings.
